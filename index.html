How to play

Pick a number from Easy, Medium or Hard, or hit Shuffle. Three famous lines from a song appear here, big enough for the whole room, translated into plain English.

Read all the lines together and think of how they go in the original language. Teams take turns in order. The team on turn, glowing on the scoreboard, guesses first.

Scoring: Easy 5 points, Medium 10 points, Hard 15 points. Each song has one hint (the movie and year). Using it halves the points, rounded up: Easy 3, Medium 5, Hard 8. Every guess has a 30 second timer. If a team misses or time runs out, the turn passes to the next team, who can steal for half points with a fresh 30 seconds. Once a song is revealed, its number is ticked off and greyed out on the board, so it can't be picked again. After the reveal, the song plays from the clue line for 1 minute.

Words by team

Every team has its own set of 5 Diwali words. On a team's turn, tap one of its glowing words to play it.

🪔🔤🪔

How to play

The team on turn picks one of its words. The word appears here all jumbled up, with a clue, and a 15 second timer starts ticking!

Shout the real word before time runs out: a correct answer scores 10 points. Miss it, or let the clock beat you, and the word passes to the next team, who get a fresh 15 seconds to steal it for 5 points. It keeps passing until someone gets it or every team has had a try.

Answered words tick off on the board. When every word is done, the team with the most points wins the dhamaka! 🏆

3 of 6One hint only, and it halves the points

Team Jalebi hums the lines back, talks it over, and shouts their guess. Stuck? The host can open the song's one hint, which reveals the movie and year. Using it halves that song's points, rounded up: an Easy song drops from 5 to 3 points, Medium from 10 to 5, Hard from 15 to 8. The reduced value applies to whichever team ends up answering the song.

When the team has guessed, right or wrong, the host taps ✅ Correct!, 😬 Missed, or 🎉 Reveal the song.
